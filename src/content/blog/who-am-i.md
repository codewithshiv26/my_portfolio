# Who Am I?

Hey! I'm Shivam, a second-year B.Tech Computer Science student specializing in Data Science.

I'm curious about technology and enjoy understanding how things work behind the scenes — from a small program to the systems that power an application.

## What I'm exploring

My current interests include backend development, IoT, programming fundamentals and data science. I'm still exploring, so this list will probably change as I learn more.

## How I approach learning

I believe concepts become clearer when I use them. I try to build small things, read documentation, make mistakes and then improve what I've made.

## Why document it?

This website gives me one place to look back at what I've learned, share useful notes and keep track of projects. It's a record of the process, not just the finished results.
